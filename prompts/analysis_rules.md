## Step 1. Analyze the Story

Analyze the story first.

Provide the analysis in **Korean** for reviewer verification.

Include the following sections:

- 등장인물 (Characters)
- 배경 (Setting)
- 주요 사건 (Major Events)
- 등장인물의 목표 (Character Goals)
- 등장인물의 감정 변화 (Character Feelings)
- 갈등 (Conflict)
- 해결 (Resolution)
- 주제 (Theme)
- 대화 화자 / 주인공 (Speaking Main Character)
- 핵심 전이 주제 (Transfer Anchors)

---

## Speaking Main Character

Identify the single character who speaks directly to the learner in first person.

This character uses "I", "me", and "my" when referring to themselves in the Opening Line and in all Recall and Inference questions.

Required:
- Name exactly one Speaking Main Character.
- Confirm that this character is the perspective through which Recall and Inference questions will be framed.

All Recall and Inference questions about this character must use first-person perspective:
- "What did I...?" not "What did [Name]...?"
- "Why was I...?" not "Why was [Name]...?"
- "How did I feel...?" not "How did [Name] feel...?"

## Transfer Anchors

Identify 2–4 core themes from the story that connect naturally to the learner's real life.

Find Transfer Anchors in:
- the main conflict
- the main character's main problem
- an important action or decision
- a meaningful character change
- the resolution
- the core lesson or message

Do not use incidental nouns, settings, or minor characters as Transfer Anchors.

### Poppy — Example

Story core:
- Poppy only eats one kind of food.
- He is afraid or unwilling to try other foods.
- He finally tries new food and finds it good.
- His attitude toward new things changes.

Good Transfer Anchors:
- trying new food
- trying something new you were afraid of
- changing your mind after trying something
- being open to new experiences

Bad Transfer Anchors:
- rabbits (Chef Pip is a rabbit — incidental character detail)
- favorite animals (the story is not about animals generally)
- friends (the picnic companions are incidental to the conflict)
- colors alone (colors appear but are not the central conflict)

### Pip — Example

Story core:
- Pip and Dot grow together and their friendship deepens through change.

Good Transfer Anchors:
- friendship
- changing and growing with a friend
- doing something new

Note: "What do you usually do with your friends?" is a reasonable Transfer question for Pip because friendship is a central theme. The same question is NOT appropriate for Poppy because friendship is incidental to Poppy's story.
