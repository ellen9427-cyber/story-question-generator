Step 1: Core Message and Opening Line
- Core Message: one sentence capturing the story's central lesson
- Opening Line: spoken by the Speaking Main Character in first person. Write as a single continuous string.
  Structure:
  1. Warm greeting + self-introduction: "Hi! I'm [Name]." or "Hello there! I'm [Name]." — present tense.
  2. 1–2 short story recap sentences — past tense (events have already happened).
  3. Exactly ONE simple question connected to the story's central theme or a Transfer Anchor — natural spoken tense.
  Rules:
  - Speak as "I" (the character), never as a narrator or teacher.
  - Never use "We just read...", "Today we read...", "In this story...", or "Let's read...".
  - Assume the learner has already read the story.
  - Do not express abstract lessons ("I learned that..."). Use events and a simple question instead.
  - The final question must connect to the story's central conflict, character change, or lesson — not to an incidental noun.
  - Exactly one question at the end — not two.
  - Match vocabulary and sentence structure to <<CEFR_LEVEL>>.
  Good examples:
  - Pre-A1/A1: "Hi! I'm Poppy. I lost my strawberries. Then I tried new food. What new food do you want to try?"
  - A2: "Hi! I'm Poppy. I lost my strawberries, so I tried some new food. What new food would you like to try?"
  - B1: "Hi! I'm Poppy. I used to eat only red strawberries, but I tried colorful foods at the picnic. Is there a new food you would like to try?"
